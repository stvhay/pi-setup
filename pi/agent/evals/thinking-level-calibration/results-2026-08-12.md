# Thinking-Level Calibration — 2026-08-12

## Decision

Keep current task routing defaults. Low/medium matched high/xhigh completion quality on seven sanitized cases, but proposed lower levels were **21.2% slower** across eligible cases instead of meeting tracked **10% reduction** gate. Single-run latency moved in both directions, so no stable latency win is established.

## Protocol

- Model: `openai-codex/gpt-5.6-sol` at low, medium, high, and xhigh.
- Cases: mechanical inventory, documentation, formatting, architecture, implementation, security, and final verification.
- Execution: cold one-shot, one provider request, 180-second deadline, inline structured output, no token/cost cap.
- Isolation: every level received byte-identical visible packet text; thinking existed only in dispatch metadata.
- Scheduling: four levels in parallel per case; seven case waves serially.
- Scoring: exact expected result/findings plus execution-dimension validation.
- Raw evidence: private `.pi/eval-runs/thinking-level-calibration/{runs,summary}.json`; not tracked.

## Interpretation

- Quality parity: passed on this sanitized replay.
- Output-token reduction: observed, but not used as quality proxy or promotion gate.
- Latency reduction: failed; proposed lower levels were slower in aggregate.
- Tool reliability and findings: parity; no tool errors, and every level found all three seeded issues.
- Routing change: none. Rubric remains available for further replay; existing task frontmatter stays authoritative.

One sample per cell cannot characterize variance. More repetitions may be useful only if future telemetry again suggests meaningful thinking-level latency savings.
