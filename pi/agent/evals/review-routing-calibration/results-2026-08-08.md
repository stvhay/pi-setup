# Terra versus Luna review-routing calibration

**Decision:** Retain `openai-codex/gpt-5.6-terra` as bounded-review primary.

## Protocol

- Four packets: two seeded reviews plus matched clean controls.
- Three paired repetitions per packet.
- Each pair ran Terra and Luna in parallel with identical packet text.
- Effective execution: `one-shot`, `thinking=high`, one provider request, 180-second deadline, inline output, no token or cost cap.
- Eligible evidence: 24 child records; all exact-payload and dimension-valid.
- Raw session, outputs, child IDs, and verification records remain private under a `0700` run directory with `0600` files.

Packet-local versioning excluded 24 pre-score pilot children after their fixtures exposed undeclared seeded/control behavior. Exclusion was based on changed packet version and exact tracked payload, not model output. One orphan assistant tool intent had no tool-result envelope or child execution and was ignored by collector contract; a regression test preserves that boundary.
